Amazon Sales Business Analytics Project

Overview

This project analyzes Amazon's sales performance, product and category performance, customer purchasing behavior, payment methods, seller revenue, and monthly revenue trends using SQL.

Tools Used

- PostgreSQL
- Excel
- Power BI

Dataset

- 100,000 records
- 100,000 unique orders

Business Questions

- Overall sales performance and Average Order Value (AOV)
- Top-selling products by quantity
- Revenue contribution and AOV by category
- Revenue and sales volume comparison across categories
- Revenue analysis by payment method
- Repeat customer and customer spending analysis
- Highest-selling products by category and city
- Top customers by spending and revenue contribution
- Customer purchasing patterns and consecutive-month purchases
- Running revenue and month-over-month growth analysis
- Second-order timing analysis
- Customer spending compared with city averages
- Top 10% of customers by spending and revenue contribution
- New vs. returning customer revenue analysis

Key Skills Demonstrated

- SQL Queries
- Aggregate Functions
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Window Functions
- Subqueries
- Joins
- Date and Time Analysis
- Customer Segmentation
- Business Analysis
- Data Interpretation
